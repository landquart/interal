The review suggestion to restore `lemma:333e7551466b9ef609d5` to `la:illos` was checked against the original record. Its **stored word is `áquellos`**, although its search form is `aquellos`; its only evidence is a candidate-index substring match to `ellos`. Spanish `aquellos` is a valid form of `aquel`, while the former accented demonstrative spelling is `aquéllos`, with the accent on the second syllable. The stored `áquellos` is therefore not reinstated without correcting and sourcing the lemma itself. References: https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/aquellos and https://www.rae.es/gram%C3%A1tica/sintaxis/clases-de-demostrativos .

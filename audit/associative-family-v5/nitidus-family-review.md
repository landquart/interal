# Latin *nitidus* family review

Source run `35647932153`. The repository had two separate IDs, `ety:03193cddd861` (`la:nitid`) and `ety:94c776d13c76` (`la:nitidus`), with identical 4,967-member arrays in English, German, French, Spanish and Italian. The first key represented a truncated stem of the second etymon. Their short `net`/`nett` aliases produced incidental substring matches in surnames, networks, misspellings and joined corpus text. Some of these records carried the legacy source label `manually_verified`; that label did not provide word-level etymological evidence.

The duplicate `la:nitid` family was removed. The `la:nitidus` family retains 18 specifically reviewed words: English `neat`, `neate`, `neater`, `neatly`; German `nett`, `nette`; French `net`, `nettement`, `netteté`, `nettoyer`, `nettoyage`, `nettoyant`, `nettoyeur`; Spanish `neto`, `nítido`; Italian `netto`, `nitido`, `nettare`. The last two previously missing words (`nítido`, `nettare`) were copied from their separate surface families with explicit reviewed evidence. The originals remain there. The unrelated `nitty` alias and dictionary relation records for excluded headwords were removed.

The English `net` member is excluded because a single word-level record cannot distinguish its clean/net-value sense from the unrelated mesh/network sense. French `net` is retained for its adjective sense, but its noun sense borrowed from English still warrants a sense-level split if the data model gains sense IDs. The family stays `needs_review` for that reason. Source examples: https://fr.wiktionary.org/wiki/net , https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/neat , https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/net , https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/nettare .

The decision ledger locks every original member list and evidence distribution. Removal of the other candidate-index links is a family-level structural decision, not an independent annotation of 9,918 excluded records. Some genuine inflected or derived forms may require separate sense and lemma review before restoration. No full index regeneration was performed.

September 29 extension: `surface:es:nitid` (six candidate-index members) was removed after its ordered digest and evidence distribution matched the new ledger; the selected `nítido` was already copied into the curated family. Three clearly unrelated reverse `net` aliases (`magnes`, `grenade`, `planet`) were removed first. A separate routing ledger quarantines 21 remaining uncurated etymon reverse targets, leaving the curated *nitidus* family and German/Italian `net` surface anchors. No other memberships were deleted by this alias decision. The retained 18 members now carry explicit review evidence, and `nitidus`, `neto`, `nitido` are additional lookup aliases. Italian *nettare* is a derivative through Latin *nitidare*: https://it.wiktionary.org/wiki/nettare . French adjective *net* is from *nitidus*: https://fr.wiktionary.org/wiki/net .

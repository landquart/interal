# Independent calibration instructions — v1

This document is a **provisional protocol**, frozen before any human answers. It cannot be called the empirically finalized procedure until the calibration and independent signoff are complete. Only packets passing the coordinator's release checks may be assigned. Preview packets are not released tasks.

You are assessing whether a specified national lexeme in a specified sense belongs to the proposed associative family. Family membership and associative recognizability are separate questions. Do not estimate Di, Pr, Sh, SWOW, A, F, P, FAv or FAₐ. Do not treat three control languages as a minimum for the existence of a family. Use the approved term “ассоциативный корень”, not “ассоциативное слово”. The unchanged approved definitions and membership/correspondence policies accompanying the packet govern the task; this instruction does not replace them.

1. Confirm the language, exact lexeme, dictionary POS, source identity, sense and context. Do not merge homonyms by spelling. Record all plausible readings if the intended corpus sense is unknown. An unresolved identity is a reason for UNCERTAIN, not removal.
2. Identify the normative root and the exact contextual national correspondence. A packet's proposed family form is a question, not an approved normative form or a previous verdict. If the normative element cannot be identified, record the gap explicitly. Do not generalize one correspondence to every similar-looking form.
3. Check the morphological or historical relation, segmentation, component role, direction and every intermediate lexeme/sense. Inflection alone does not establish derivation. For compounds, establish the relevant components and meaning. A shared string, distant ancestry, or arbitrary semantic propagation is insufficient.
4. Record sources with publisher, version/date, precise entry/row/page locator, URL or stable archive reference, the observed finding, its relevance to this exact sense, and source dependence where known. Imported morphology rows are evidence to examine, not an answer key. Seek alternatives and contradictions. Source popularity or number of dependent databases is not a vote.
5. Answer VALID only when a sufficient morphosemantic path establishes this exact membership. Answer INVALID only with an independent sufficient scoped reason excluding it, such as a documented incompatible identity or refuted component analysis. Absence of supporting evidence is not negative proof. Membership in another family does not exclude this one. Otherwise answer UNCERTAIN and state which fact remains missing.
6. Complete every response field. Use explicit null for genuinely unknown scientific fields in an UNCERTAIN response and explain it; never invent an ID, sense or citation. Dates must include a timezone. Record start/finish and active seconds honestly, excluding pauses from active time. Report confusing rule wording in rule_issues, even if you reached a verdict.
7. Work independently. Do not consult the other reviewer, algorithm predictions, old decisions, coordinator category/risk labels, or any intended answer. Report accidental exposure immediately. Do not replace an earlier answer silently; corrections require an append-only amendment and a new review round.

Required response fields: schema, study_sha256, protocol_sha256, question_id, question_sha256, actor_id, role A/B, action independent_review, lexeme, language, pos, sense, normative_root, national_correspondence, morphological_relation, verdict VALID/INVALID/UNCERTAIN, grounds, sources, rule_issues, started_at, completed_at, active_seconds, blind_to_predictions=true, blind_to_other_review=true, exposure_report=null or a description.

For a substantive result, sources must document the exact lexical/sense/context relationship, not merely a lemma search result. A reviewer may disagree with a source, algorithm, or the proposed family; original qualified responses are retained without requiring agreement with model predictions. Calibration labels are research observations and do not bypass normative, historical, lexical-binding, quality or release gates.

An independent third expert reviews every difference in verdict, lexeme, language, POS, sense, root, national correspondence or relation, every uncertainty pair, and the preregistered random 10% agreement audit. The expert sees both completed initial responses only after they are locked, supplies an independent reasoned response, and references both exact response hashes. Arbitration never substitutes for either initial review. Any substantive instruction clarification triggers a new version and calibration round; the reserved evaluation holdout stays untouched.

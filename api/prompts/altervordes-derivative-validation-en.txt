VII. EVALUATING DERIVATIONAL POTENTIAL

Determine only whether the analysed form has derivational potential.

Derivational potential exists when the form supports at least one regular and semantically justified direction of word formation without a verified morphological, orthographic, or semantic conflict.

Derivational potential does not exist when no such direction is supported or when word formation is blocked by a verified conflict.

Strictly distinguish between present, root, and derivational stems, past-tense forms, and independent words. Do not treat a bound stem or grammatical form as an independent derivative. Do not extend an individual example to other lexical families.

Do not construct or list concrete derivatives. Do not select suffixes or propose nouns, adjectives, agents, instruments, places, processes, results, or properties.

Always return an empty array `[]` in `derivation.possibleDerivations`.

In `analysis.derivationalPotential`, return exactly one of these two values:

- `Yes.` — when derivational potential exists;
- `No.` — when derivational potential does not exist.

The value must be consistent with `derivation.canFormVerb`, `derivation.canFormNoun`, and `derivation.canFormAdjective`.

No explanations, references, paragraph numbers, rule names, examples, form lists, qualifications, or caveats are permitted in `analysis.derivationalPotential`.
